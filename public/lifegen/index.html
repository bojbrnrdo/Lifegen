Our story

A generation that seeks Me.

LIFEGEN — Life Generation 2026 — is Lifegiver Church of Faith (Central)'s home for people learning to live a life like Jesus. We gather every Sunday from 1:00 to 3:00 PM: one family, one table, one great harvest. The doors are open — and the invitation is personal.
